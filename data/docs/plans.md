# Plans and pricing

The Basic plan costs $30 per month and includes 5 GB of high-speed data.
The Plus plan costs $45 per month and includes 20 GB of high-speed data.
The Unlimited plan costs $60 per month and includes unlimited high-speed data.

Data used beyond the plan allowance is charged an overage fee of $10 per GB.
Customers who enroll in autopay receive a discount of $5 per month on any plan.
