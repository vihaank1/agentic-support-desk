# Troubleshooting

If the phone shows no service, restart the phone first and wait two minutes for it to reconnect.
If restarting does not help, remove and reseat the SIM card, then restart again.
To reset network settings, open Settings, choose General or System, then Reset, then Reset Network Settings.
If mobile data is not working but calls work, check that the APN settings match the carrier defaults.
Check the carrier outage page before changing settings, because a local outage cannot be fixed on the phone.
